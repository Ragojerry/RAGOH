# RAGOH — Guide for data collectors

Short and practical. Keep it on your phone or print it.

---

## 1. Before your first day

1. **Install the app.** Your supervisor gives you the RAGOH file (an "APK"). Open it and tap **Install**. If the phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
2. **Sign in.** Use the username and password your supervisor gave you. The first sign-in needs internet.
3. **Read the privacy notice.** It explains what RAGOH records, when, who can see it and how long it is kept. Tap **I have read this and agree**. If you don't agree, talk to your supervisor first. You can read it again any time: **Settings → Read the privacy notice**.
4. **Create a PIN** (4–6 digits). You use it to open the app, even without internet. After 5 wrong tries you must sign in again.
5. **Choose your language** in **Settings → Language** (words not yet translated stay in English).
6. **Allow location "All the time"** when the phone asks. Without it, your route is not recorded and your work cannot be confirmed.
7. **Stop the phone from killing RAGOH.** The first time you start a shift, the app opens the battery settings: find RAGOH and choose **Don't optimise / No restrictions / Allow**. On Tecno, Infinix and itel phones also allow **Auto-start** and lock RAGOH in the recent-apps screen.
8. **Download the offline map** (on Wi-Fi if you can): **Settings → Download offline map**.

## 2. Every working day

### Start
1. Turn on **Location (GPS)** and mobile data.
2. Open RAGOH → enter your PIN → **Start shift**.
3. You will see a notice "RAGOH is recording your location" while the shift is on. Location is recorded **only** during your shift.

### Plan your route
1. **My points** shows today's points on the map and as a list.
2. Tap **Plan my route** for the shortest walking order. Numbers on the map show the order.
3. Tap a point → **Walking route** for directions to it, or **Open in Maps**.

### At each point
1. Tap the point → **Start: <survey name>** (or choose **Not found**, **Refused** or **Skip for now** if you can't do it — always give the reason).
2. Fill the form. Required questions are marked. Take photos when asked.
3. Tap **Finish and save**. The survey is saved on the phone at once. An unfinished survey is kept as a draft: **Continue: <survey name>**.
4. Do the interview **at the household**. RAGOH compares where and when the survey was filled with your route, and checks if visits were too short or done from far away.

### Sync
- RAGOH sends your work by itself every few minutes when there is network. You can also tap **Sync now**.
- **Nothing is lost without network.** Work waits on the phone and is sent later. The home screen shows how many records are waiting.
- If a record is **refused by the server**, open **My day** to see why and tell your supervisor.

### Messages and questions
- **Messages** shows notes from your supervisor. When your supervisor asks you to **explain** something about your work, answer with **Send explanation** — keep it factual.
- A **revisit** or **back-check** task appears in your points list with a label.

### End
1. Sync once more if you can.
2. Tap **End shift**. Recording stops. Your work stays on the phone until it is sent.

## 3. No network for a long time?

Your supervisor can carry your work to the office on their phone:
1. Home → **No network? Hand data to supervisor** → **Show codes**.
2. Hold your phone still with the screen bright. Your supervisor scans the codes with their camera (a few seconds).
3. Tap **My supervisor has it**.

Your work is locked so only the server can read it. It also stays on your phone and is sent again when you sync — it is never counted twice.

## 4. Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Location tracking is not running" | Turn on GPS. Check RAGOH has location **All the time**. Check battery settings (step 1.7). Then open the app again. |
| Phone restarted during the shift | Open RAGOH: tracking starts again. |
| Forgot PIN | After 5 tries you are signed out. Sign in again with your password (needs internet) and make a new PIN. |
| "This phone has been blocked" | Call your supervisor. |
| App shows "Something went wrong" | Tap **Try again**. Your work is saved. If it keeps happening, tell your supervisor — the problem is reported automatically. |
| Battery runs out fast | Lower screen brightness, close other apps, carry a power bank. Don't turn off GPS during the shift. |
| Lost or stolen phone | Tell your supervisor **at once**. They can lock and erase the phone remotely. |

## 5. Your data and your rights
- RAGOH records your location **only during your shift**, plus the surveys you fill.
- Your supervisor and programme managers for your area can see it. Every time someone looks at your route or downloads data, it is recorded.
- Detailed location points are deleted after **90 days** (unless your programme set another period — the privacy notice in the app shows the current one).
- You can ask to see or correct your data: ask your supervisor or the programme's data protection officer.
